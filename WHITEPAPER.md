
# GBitcoin (GBTC)

**Version:** 1.0  
**Network:** GRAM

## 1. Abstract

GBitcoin (GBTC) is designed around a multi-project ecosystem concept that aims to connect independent digital assets, projects, and services through shared infrastructure, liquidity relationships, and interoperable trading opportunities.

The project's central idea is to create an environment in which different projects can maintain their own identities, objectives, communities, and utilities while participating in a broader connected ecosystem.

Rather than treating each project as an isolated environment, GBitcoin explores a framework for cooperation, shared participation, and interconnected digital asset activity.

**One protocol. Multiple projects. Shared growth.**

## 2. Vision

The vision of GBitcoin is to contribute to a more connected digital asset ecosystem on GRAM.

The project aims to support an environment where independent initiatives can interact through liquidity pools, trading pairs, and future ecosystem services.

This approach seeks to encourage collaboration, improve access to connected markets, and create opportunities for projects to participate in a wider network without sacrificing their independence.

## 3. Core Concept

GBitcoin is based on a multi-project connectivity model.

Each participating project may retain:

- Its own token and economic model.
- Its own purpose and utility.
- Its own community and identity.
- Its own development direction.
- Its own independent market dynamics.

The ecosystem is intended to provide shared points of interaction through which these projects can become connected.

## 4. Multi-Project Liquidity

Liquidity is an important starting point for the GBitcoin concept.

The framework explores the use of liquidity pools and trading pairs to connect different digital assets.

These connections may enable:

- Trading between participating assets.
- Additional market access.
- Greater interaction between independent projects.
- Shared liquidity opportunities where supported by pool design.
- A foundation for future interconnected services.

Liquidity arrangements depend on the assets, pool configurations, available capital, and market participation.

The existence of a connected pool does not guarantee liquidity depth, stable prices, or protection against losses.

## 5. Independent Assets and Shared Infrastructure

GBitcoin aims to distinguish between project independence and ecosystem connectivity.

Participating projects are not required to share the same token economics, governance structure, or development objectives.

Instead, the ecosystem concept focuses on creating additional opportunities for interaction while respecting the individual characteristics of each asset.

Shared infrastructure should not be interpreted as shared ownership or a guarantee of financial support between participating projects.

The long-term direction of GBitcoin extends beyond liquidity connections.

## 9. Security and Risk Considerations

Digital asset participation involves technical, financial, and operational risks.

These may include:

- Smart contract vulnerabilities.
- Liquidity shortages.
- Market volatility.
- Price fluctuations and slippage.
- Blockchain network interruptions.
- Third-party protocol risks.
- Regulatory and legal uncertainty.

Connecting assets through liquidity pools does not eliminate these risks or guarantee that one asset will support the market value of another.

Users should conduct independent research and assess risks before participating in any digital asset activity.

## 10. Roadmap Philosophy

GBitcoin's development is intended to progress through a practical and gradual approach.

The general development direction includes:

1. Establishing the project's identity and documentation.
2. Developing the multi-project ecosystem concept.
3. Exploring liquidity connections and trading infrastructure.
4. Expanding project collaboration opportunities.
5. Evaluating additional ecosystem services and applications.

Specific features, timelines, and integrations remain subject to technical feasibility, resources, and development decisions.

## 11. Transparency and Documentation

## 12. Conclusion

GBitcoin represents a multi-project ecosystem approach focused on connectivity, collaboration, and shared infrastructure.

By exploring relationships between independent digital assets through liquidity and future services, the project aims to contribute to a more interconnected environment on GRAM.

Its broader direction is based on the principle that independent projects can preserve their identities while discovering new opportunities through structured cooperation.

**One protocol. Multiple projects. Shared growth.**
