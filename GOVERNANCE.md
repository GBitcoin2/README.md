
# Governance

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the governance principles and decision-making approach of the GBitcoin (GBTC) project.

Its purpose is to promote transparency, accountability, consistency, and responsible project development.

## 2. Governance Principles

GBitcoin aims to follow these principles:

- Transparency in project-related decisions.
- Open and constructive technical discussion.
- Responsible management of project resources and repositories.
- Clear documentation of significant changes.
- Respect for contributors and community feedback.
- Security, reliability, and long-term sustainability.

## 3. Project Direction

The project's direction is guided by its documented objectives, technical requirements, and long-term development goals.

Proposed changes should support the project's purpose and avoid misleading claims, unnecessary complexity, or unsupported functionality.

## 4. Proposals and Discussions

Anyone may submit suggestions, report issues, or propose improvements through the project's available communication and development channels.

Proposals should:

- Clearly explain the suggested change.
- Describe its purpose and expected benefits.
- Identify potential risks or limitations.
- Include supporting technical information when relevant.

Significant proposals may require further discussion before a decision is made.

## 5. Decision-Making

Project decisions should consider:

- Technical quality and feasibility.
- Security and reliability.
- Compatibility with existing project components.
- Long-term maintenance requirements.
- Community feedback where appropriate.

Final decisions concerning project-managed repositories and official project materials are made by the individuals authorized to maintain those resources.

Community feedback is valuable, but participation or contribution does not automatically grant decision-making authority.

## 6. Contributions and Reviews

Contributions are subject to review before being accepted into official project repositories.

Reviewers may request modifications, clarification, additional testing, or supporting documentation.

Changes should follow the project's contribution guidelines and security practices.

## 7. Security and Responsible Disclosure

Security-related concerns should be reported in accordance with the procedures described in `SECURITY.md`.

Potential vulnerabilities should be handled responsibly, with priority given to protecting users, project infrastructure, and ecosystem participants.

## 8. Changes to Governance

This governance document may be revised as the project evolves.

Updates should be documented clearly and published through the official project repository.

## 9. Limitations

This document describes the project's governance principles and does not establish a formal legal entity, decentralized autonomous organization (DAO), voting mechanism, or independent governing council.

No voting rights, financial rights, or ownership interests are created by participation in the project.

## 10. Transparency and Accountability

The project aims to maintain clear documentation of important changes and communicate relevant updates through its official channels.

All project-related statements should be accurate, responsible, and consistent with the project's actual status and capabilities.
