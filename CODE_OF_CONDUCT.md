
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The GBitcoin (GBTC) project is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, transparent, and constructive environment for everyone participating in its ecosystem.

We welcome individuals from different backgrounds, technical disciplines, and levels of experience who wish to contribute to the development and improvement of the project.

Participation in this project is expected to reflect respect, integrity, accountability, and a shared commitment to constructive collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Treat community members with respect and professionalism.
- Communicate clearly, honestly, and constructively.
- Welcome reasonable questions, feedback, and technical discussions.
- Respect different perspectives and technical approaches.
- Provide accurate information and avoid misleading statements.
- Focus discussions on ideas, contributions, and project improvements.
- Respect the time, work, and contributions of others.
- Report security concerns through appropriate responsible disclosure channels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following conduct is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Threats, abusive language, or deliberate attempts to disrupt discussions.
- Publishing private or confidential information without permission.
- Impersonating project representatives or misrepresenting official communications.
- Spreading knowingly false information about the project or its contributors.
- Manipulating discussions, contributions, or review processes through deceptive practices.
- Any behavior intended to create a hostile or unsafe environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project-related spaces and interactions, including:

- GitHub repositories, issues, and pull requests.
- Technical discussions and development channels.
- Official project communication channels.
- Other spaces where individuals are representing or directly participating in the GBitcoin project.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it through the project's official communication channels.

Reports should include relevant details and supporting information whenever possible.

All reports should be handled with appropriate discretion and reviewed fairly.

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Conduct that violates these principles may result in appropriate action, depending on the nature and severity of the incident.

Possible actions include:

- A formal warning.
- Removal of inappropriate content.
- Temporary restrictions on participation.
- Permanent exclusion from project-managed spaces.

Decisions should be proportionate to the circumstances and made with fairness and consistency.

## Good-Faith Participation

We encourage constructive criticism, technical disagreement, independent research, and open discussion.

Disagreement itself is not a violation of this Code. Participants are expected to address disagreements respectfully and focus on evidence, reasoning, and practical solutions.

## Responsibility

Every participant shares responsibility for helping maintain a respectful and productive environment.

By contributing to or participating in GBitcoin project spaces, individuals are expected to follow these principles.

## Updates

This Code of Conduct may be updated as the GBitcoin project and its community evolve.

The latest version published in the official project repository takes precedence over previous versions.
